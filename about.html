About The AI Business Guide

Our aim is to make business technology easier to understand.

We create practical guides to AI tools, software and automation for freelancers, consultants and small businesses. Our goal is to help readers compare options and make informed decisions based on their needs and budgets.

As this website grows, we will publish our editorial approach and explain how products are researched and evaluated. We will not claim to have tested a product unless we have actually done so.
